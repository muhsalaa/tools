# GLOSSARY

## Tool

An independent mini-app on the home grid. Own route, own icon. Ex: Pomodoro, Notes.

## Session

One completed focus block in Pomodoro. Has start, end, duration. Interrupted blocks not Sessions.

## DayLog

Today's Sessions list. Local date. Purged on date change. No carry.

## NoteCard

One temp note. Title optional, body text, createdAt. Manual delete only. No sync.
